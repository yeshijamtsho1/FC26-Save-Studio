**Tactical Data Sculptor** is not merely another file editor—it is a precision instrument for the discerning manager who sees beyond the ninety minutes. This repository is designed for the user who understands that true customization is not a luxury—it's a necessity. ## 🚀 Getting Started: Your First Alteration

Before you dive into the granular details, it's crucial to set up your workspace. The setup process is designed to be as frictionless as possible, allowing you to transition from download to data manipulation in a matter of minutes.

**FieldForge** is a self-contained application, requiring no external package installation or command-line acrobatics. Once you have obtained the latest release, you simply unzip the archive to your desired location. The application will automatically detect your primary FC 26 installation directory, but you can override this setting within the configuration menu if you manage multiple sandbox environments.

The initial launch presents a dashboard that serves as your command center. From here, you can access the four primary modules, each acting as a specialized lens for viewing and modifying the game's core data structures. The interface is designed to be responsive, adapting fluidly to different screen resolutions, ensuring that whether you are on a compact laptop or a multi-monitor setup, your toolkit remains perfectly legible and accessible.

### Core Modules

Our architecture is segmented into distinct, isolated modules to prevent data corruption and ensure user-friendly navigation. These modules are the pillars of your editing experience.

- **The Transfer Ledger (Database Changes):** This module is your financial nexus. It allows you to manipulate the intricate fiscal and club-level data. Adjust transfer budgets, modify sponsorship deals, balance the wage bills, or completely restructure league rules. It's not about breaking the game's economy; it's about tuning it to your preferred difficulty or fantasy scenario.
- **The Player Forge (Player Stats):** Here, you can delve into the physical and technical minutiae of any player in the database. This is more than just sliding a "pace" bar. You can adjust specific acceleration curves, refine sprint frequency in tactical situations, tweak ball control in adverse weather conditions, and alter mentals like "tactical awareness" or "big match temperament." The granularity is unmatched.
- **The Timeline Forge (Career Mode):** This is your narrative control. From editing the starting season date to modifying fixture congestion, contractual clauses, and player growth curves (or decline curves for veterans), this module allows you to shape the long-term narrative arc of your save. You have the power to ensure a club legend never declines or to introduce a dynamic youth regeneration system.
- **The Live Data Pulse (Live Data Updates):** For those who play beyond the static save file, this module reads and writes to the dynamic data layers. This includes real-world form updates, dynamic morale modifiers based on match results, and the shifting market values that fluctuate with player performance. It allows for a more organic, living simulation that responds to the on-pitch action.

## 🗺️ Navigation & Interface

The interface is built with a "less is more" philosophy, yet it is packed with powerful tools. The left-hand sidebar acts as a persistent anchor, providing immediate access to the modules. The central panel displays your selected data table, while the right panel offers contextual properties. This three-pane setup maximizes efficiency.

- **Tabular Data View:** The core data is displayed in advanced, sortable tables. You can filter by club, league, nationality, or any other meta-criterion. The tables support multi-select and batch editing, so you can apply a +2 overall increase to an entire youth academy with a single action.
- **Deep Search Functionality:** A powerful search engine powers the entire application. You can type in a player's name, a club's ID, or even a specific facial hair style. The search index is built for speed, and results are instant.
- **Visual Feedback Loop:** Every change you make triggers a visual update in a dynamic "squad rating" calculator at the bottom of the screen. This tool instantly recalculates team strength, finances, and league position predictions based on your current edits, so you can see the ripple effects of your changes before you commit.

## 🔧 Advanced Features

Beyond the basic sliders and text fields, **FieldForge** houses a suite of professional-grade tools.

- **Import/Export Integrity:** The tool supports robust batch import/export. You can export your custom player data sets or tactical formations to share with your community, or import third-party data patches—ours or others—to further expand the boundaries of the simulation.
- **Manual Data Backup:** Before any significant session, the application automatically generates a manual backup of your original game files, timestamped for easy rollback. This ensures a safety net is always in place, allowing you to experiment without fear.
- **Cross-Save Compatibility:** Thanks to our cloud-sync architecture (which requires no permission hacks, just user consent), you can back up your edited profiles locally and share them across different PC configurations. Your data belongs to you, and you can move it freely.
- **The Sandbox Preset:** We offer a "Simulation Mode" that lets you test *how* your file modifications will perform in a simulated CPU match engine. This is a predictive analytics tool, not a guarantee, but it helps you balance a player's stats against real-world match ratings.
